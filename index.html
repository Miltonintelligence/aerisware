Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 8, 2026

AerisWare ("we," "us," "our") sells made-to-order dog-themed gifts through our Etsy shop. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By purchasing from or interacting with our shop, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

  • Order information you provide through Etsy when you buy: your name, shipping address, email address, order details, and any personalization text you submit.
  • Messages you send us (for example, customer-service questions through Etsy).
  • Limited usage data from marketing platforms such as Pinterest — for example, aggregate stats about how our pins and links perform. This is not tied to your identity by us.
  • We do not collect or store your payment card details. Payments are processed securely by Etsy and its payment providers.

How we use your information

  • To produce, fulfill, and ship your orders.
  • To communicate with you about your order and respond to your questions.
  • To comply with our legal and tax obligations.
  • To operate and improve our shop, and — where permitted — to market our products.

How we share information

We share personal information only as needed to run the shop:

  • Etsy — our marketplace and payment platform. Etsy's own Privacy Policy governs data you provide on Etsy.
  • Printify and its print providers — our fulfillment partners, who receive your name, shipping address, and order details to produce and ship your item.
  • Shipping carriers — to deliver your order.
  • Pinterest and similar platforms — used for our marketing; their own policies apply to your activity on those platforms.
  • Authorities — if required by law, or to protect our rights.

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies & analytics

Our Etsy listings and any marketing platforms we use may set cookies and gather analytics under their own policies. We use this only in aggregate to understand what products and content are popular.

Data retention

We keep order information only as long as needed to fulfill your order, provide support, and meet legal, accounting, and tax requirements, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under GDPR in the EU/UK and the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to object to certain uses. To make a request, contact us using the details below and we will respond as required by applicable law.

Children

Our shop is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

International transfers

Because our fulfillment and marketing partners may operate in different countries, your information may be processed outside your country of residence. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Effective date" above reflects the latest version, and material changes will be posted here.

Contact us

Questions or requests about your privacy? Reach us through the "Message Seller" button on our AerisWare Etsy shop, or by email at miltonintelligence@gmail.com.
